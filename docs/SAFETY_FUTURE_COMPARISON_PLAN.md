# Frozen safety-stock comparator on M5 days 1914–1941

This **supplement** to [the original future-period protocol](NEW_TIME_HOLDOUT_PLAN.md) is committed before any successful replay of days 1914–1941. The new comparator was motivated by [inventory OR/RL benchmark repositories](RELATED_WORK.md) **after** earlier days 1801–1913 had been inspected. On those earlier days its profit point estimate exceeded the TX_3 actor, although the paired interval crossed zero; that retrospective comparison is disclosed separately and is **not** a new holdout result. The additional days 1914–1941 are the first new time period for this comparator in this project.

## Locked TX_3 comparator

- Use the safety-stock scoring code in `src/inventory_rl/m5_safety_stock.py`, with a static premium estimated **only** from the final 365 training days ending at M5 day 1700. Compute the empirical 0.70 quantile of rolling `(lead_time + 2)`-day sales sums, subtract mean sales over the same horizon, and floor at zero. On each decision day add this premium to the recent-seven-day mean sales target with cover `2`, score exponent `beta=1.0`, and the same 0/4/8/16 packs and shared budget/capacity allocator. These are the parameter values selected by the 48-candidate grid on days 1701–1800; do not retune on any later day.
- Compare the frozen safety rule with the already released TX_3 context actor (model SHA-256 `f7abb861d378a6eecc557a3a371ad2de726a32fee1b0ef093a57bde7ed9755e5`) and its frozen validation-selected strong rule. Keep original synthetic economics and SKU order. Check the official new-file MD5, old-file SHA-256 and identical days 1–1913 prefixes as in the original future protocol.
- Replay all three policies **continuously** from M5 day 1801, carrying each policy's own stock and pipeline into the new time period. Require safety rule's warm-up daily profits to exactly match its published retrospective report; actor and original rule must match their published daily profits. Score only days 1914–1941. Publish total simulated profit, fill rate, daily spend, 10th-percentile daily profit, paired daily series, and paired 7-day circular moving-block 95% intervals for safety versus actor and safety versus original rule.

Report all results regardless of sign. No model or baseline is reselected after this check. The 28-day sample and M5 sales-as-demand proxy limit interpretation; even a positive comparison would not demonstrate real retailer profit or deployment readiness.
